WhatsApp chat and voice calls, inside every Odoo contact

Spoki brings your WhatsApp Business inbox into Odoo. Open a chat or start a WhatsApp call from any contact, without leaving Odoo.

What you get

Embedded WhatsApp chat

Click Open Spoki Chat on a contact and the conversation opens right in the form, loaded from the contact's mobile or phone number. Reply exactly as you would in Spoki.

WhatsApp voice calls

Call the contact on WhatsApp from the same window. Your browser asks for microphone access the first time.

One-click setup

No keys to copy. An administrator clicks Connect to Spoki once, picks the Spoki account and the whole Odoo database is ready.

Works on Odoo Online

A data-only module with no Python code: it installs on Odoo Online (odoo.com), Odoo.sh and on-premise, Community and Enterprise.

Set it up in a minute

  1. Install this module.
  2. Go to Settings > Spoki and click Connect to Spoki.
  3. In the Spoki window, log in, choose your Spoki account and click Connect. The window closes by itself.
  4. Open any contact and click Open Spoki Chat.

Good to know

  • You need a Spoki account with an active WhatsApp Business number.
  • Each Odoo user can open chats if their email is a user of the connected Spoki account, or is not used in Spoki yet (Spoki then adds them as a chat operator).
  • Chats use the contact's Mobile or Phone field, so make sure it holds a valid international number.
  • Spoki never sends messages on its own: only your agents and your Spoki automations do.
